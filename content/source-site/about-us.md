# About Parkezi

## **Every car park is unique. Your parking system should be too**

We design and deliver custom-built parking software that can be configured and developed to suit your site’s unique challenges. Not force you into a rigid, off the shelf system.

Whether you need specialised access rules, tailored validation workflows, integrations with third-party platforms, or a completely bespoke operating model, Parkezi gives you the flexibility to build a solution that fits your environment now and evolves as your needs change.

We work alongside property owners, facility managers, and parking operators to deliver solutions that improve day-to-day operations without disrupting established workflows.

[What is ticketless parking?](https://www.parkezi.com.au/about-us/#)

Tickless parking refers to a system where drivers can park their vehicles without the need to obtain a physical ticket or permit. Instead of traditional tickets, the system uses technology like automatic number plate recognition (ANPR) to identify vehicles as they enter and leave parking facilities.

**Here's a simplified way it usually works:**

**Entry:** As the vehicle enters the parking lot, its license plate is scanned logging the vehicle's entry time.

**Duration:** The parking system monitors how long the vehicle remains in the parking facility.

**Exit:** When the vehicle leaves, its exit time is recorded using the same identification method. The system calculates the parking fee based on the duration of the stay.

**Payment:** Drivers pay for their parking time via a credit card reader or online payment system

[Permanent parkers vs casual parkers](https://www.parkezi.com.au/about-us/#)

**Permanent Parkers:** Individuals who park regularly or long-term, typically holding monthly or annual passes for consistent access. Examples include employees of nearby businesses or residents with reserved parking spaces. This is managed via a cloud-based portal by the parking management company.

‍ **Casual Parkers:** Individuals who park occasionally, paying for each visit based on time spent parked. Parkezi software can calculate time spent onsite and then charge using a dynamic pricing structure
